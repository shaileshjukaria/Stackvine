FAQ

Common Questions

How long does an MVP take? +
Typically 4–6 weeks from kick-off to deployment. We run weekly sprints with live previews so you always know exactly where things stand.
Do you work with non-technical founders? +
Absolutely — most of our clients are non-technical. We translate product goals into technical specs and keep you updated in plain English throughout the build.
What tech stack do you use? +
Our default stack is React + Node.js + MongoDB (MERN), but we also work with PostgreSQL, TypeScript, Docker, and AWS. We pick what fits the project.
What's included in the discovery call? +
A free 30-minute session where we understand your product, suggest a stack, scope the MVP, and give you a rough timeline and cost estimate. No commitment required.
Do you offer post-launch support? +
Yes — every project includes 30 days of post-launch support at no extra cost. After that, we offer affordable monthly retainers for ongoing maintenance.
How do payments work? +
We typically split into 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. For longer projects, we use milestone-based billing. We accept bank transfers, UPI, and international payments.
